Elucid, an AI medical technology company focused on providing physicians with a more precise view of atherosclerosis to drive patient-specific therapeutic decisions, has made two additions to its Board of Directors. The company named Richard E. Kuntz, MD, MSc and Shamiram Feinglass, MD, MPH as board directors.
