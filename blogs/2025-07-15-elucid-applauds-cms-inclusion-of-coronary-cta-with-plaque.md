Elucid today released a statement commending U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) for the inclusion of a Category I CPT code for coronary plaque analysis and a national Medicare average payment amount of over $1,000, pending the final Medicare Physician Fee Schedule (PFS) ruling, which will come out in November.
